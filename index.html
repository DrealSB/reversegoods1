What we pick up

We take clean, gently used items. If you’re not sure, include it in your photos — we’ll let you know.

Clothing

Tops, jeans, jackets, dresses and outerwear for all ages.

Shoes

Sneakers, boots, sandals and dress shoes with plenty of life left.

Accessories

Bags, purses, belts, hats, scarves and similar items.

Books

Hardcovers and paperbacks without water damage or missing pages.

Golf clubs

Individual clubs, partial or full sets, and golf bags.

Small household goods

Kitchenware, décor, small working gadgets, linens and more.

What we can’t take

To keep pickups quick and free, we have to pass on:

  • Furniture
  • Large appliances
  • Trash or junk
  • Damaged, stained or unusable items

About Reverse Goods

Reverse Goods is a small, local resale business based in Puyallup. We pick up gently used items people no longer need and resell them, so good things get another life instead of sitting in a closet or ending up in a landfill.

You get an easy, free way to clear space. We’ll handle the hauling and sorting. We are not a charity, so we can’t provide donation receipts.

Your information

Pickup request privacy notice

When you submit a pickup request, Reverse Goods LLC receives the contact details, pickup address, item descriptions, photos, and scheduling preferences you provide. We use these details to review your items, respond to your request, and coordinate an approved pickup.

Our request form is provided through Tally, which processes and stores submissions on our behalf. See Tally’s privacy information for details about its service.

The form requests permission to contact you about your pickup request. It does not enroll you in marketing messages. Please do not include financial details, identification documents, or other sensitive information in your photos or notes.

To ask about access to or deletion of your submission, email support@shopreversegoods.com.
