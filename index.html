About Reverse Goods

Good things deserve a second life. Reverse Goods LLC is a local, recommerce business based in Auburn, Washington. We pick up approved, gently used goods across our service area.

We’re committed to giving 15% of our net profit to nonprofits and charitable foundations. Reverse Goods is not a nonprofit or charity, and giving us items for refurbishing is not a tax-deductible charitable donation.

What we can pick up

We offer free pickup of gently used household goods and clothing that someone else could use. Have at least one full bag or box ready. A group photo is fine if we can see what you’re offering.

Clothing

Tops, jeans, jackets, dresses and outerwear for all ages.

Shoes

Wearable pairs of sneakers, boots, sandals or dress shoes.

Accessories

Bags, purses, belts, hats and scarves.

Books

Hardcovers and paperbacks without water damage or missing pages.

Golf clubs

Individual clubs, partial or full sets, and golf bags.

Small household goods

Kitchenware, décor, small working gadgets and linens.

Items we pass on

Furniture, large appliances, trash, and anything stained, damaged, incomplete or unusable.

How porch pickup works

  1. Send your photos

    Choose your area, describe your items and upload photos in the pickup form.

  2. Get your pickup window

    We’ll review your photos and use your preferred contact method to confirm what we can take and when we can come. A requested day isn’t a reservation.

  3. Set your items out

    After confirmation, bag or box your approved items, protect anything fragile and put it all at the agreed spot before your pickup window.

Pickup questions

Is porch pickup always free?

Yes. We never ask for payment, fuel contributions, transportation fees or tips. Every pickup is subject to item and route approval.

How much do I need for a pickup?

Have at least one full bag or box of gently used items ready. A request can include several accepted categories.

When is my pickup scheduled?

Your pickup is booked only once we confirm the approved items, date and time window with you. Please wait for confirmation before setting items outside.

Can I get a charitable donation receipt?

No. Reverse Goods LLC is a for-profit business, not a charity. Items we collect may be resold and do not qualify as tax-deductible charitable donations.

What condition do items need to be in?

Items must be clean, gently used, complete and usable, without significant stains, tears, odors, damage or missing components.

How should I prepare for pickup?

Set aside only approved items. Bag or box clothing, shoes and books, protect fragile pieces and use the agreed pickup spot.

What if some items aren’t approved?

We’ll let you know what we can take. We may leave behind items that don’t match the photos or aren’t in suitable condition.

Pickup request privacy notice

Tally processes and stores the contact information, pickup address, item descriptions, photos and scheduling preferences you submit. Reverse Goods uses this information to review and respond to your request and coordinate an approved pickup.

Submitting a request does not enroll you in marketing messages. Please keep financial details, ID documents, gate codes and other sensitive information out of photos and notes.

Optional advertising tracking

If you allow advertising tracking, Meta Pixel measures website visits and successful embedded pickup form submissions to help us understand Facebook and Instagram ad performance. Meta may receive cookie identifiers, IP address, browser or device details, page URLs and event names. Our tracking code does not send pickup form answers, contact details, addresses or photos. You can decline or change your preference at any time without affecting pickup eligibility. We honor Global Privacy Control for this tracking. Read Meta’s privacy policy.

For access or deletion requests, email support@shopreversegoods.com. Read Tally’s privacy policy.
